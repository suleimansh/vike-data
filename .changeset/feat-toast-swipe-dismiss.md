Toast: swipe to dismiss (Sonner's flick). Drag a toast toward its region edge past a threshold to close it, or a shorter drag snaps it back. The direction follows the deck's position (down for a bottom deck, up for a top one); the toast follows the finger and fades as it goes. Works with mouse, touch, and pen via Pointer Events, dep-free, with the drag math shared by the React and Vue Toasters so the twins can't drift.
